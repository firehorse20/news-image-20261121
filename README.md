One image summing up the world's news of Saturday 21 November 2026, as a
newspaper front page: Mexico captures FBI most-wanted cartel boss Fausto
Isidro Meza Flores ("El Chapo Isidro") in Sinaloa; a Russian ballistic
missile hits Kryvyi Rih, Zelenskyy's hometown; Trump weighs new strikes on
Iran; Anthropic debuts on Nasdaq as a roughly $2 trillion company; the
S&P 500 fell 3.7% and the Nikkei 5.06% in an AI sell-off; COP31 runs past
its deadline in Antalya; the World Court rules Myanmar committed genocide
against the Rohingya; Russian missiles land in Poland.

The page is drawn from HTML/CSS and rendered to `entry.png` (1024×1024) in
headless Chromium — no borrowed photos, all type and rules are its own.
`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`).

Sources: AP, NYT, Reuters, BBC, The Guardian.
